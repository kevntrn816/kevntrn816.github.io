Hinge protection

Covered through every fold.

Explore its development history

Spigen has protected foldable hinges since 2019, starting with one of the first protective cases for the original Galaxy Fold [confirm]. On iPhone Duo, the hinge cover moves with the fold, and a spring cartridge built into the hinge pin (patent pending) pulls it back into place, so it stays tight against the phone open or closed.

Air Cushion

Air takes the hit.

Explore its development history

Air Cushion has been in Spigen corners since 2013. When a drop lands, hollow pockets inside each corner compress first, spreading the force into the case walls instead of the phone. On iPhone Duo, both halves get the same corners [confirm], so it’s covered whether it lands open or closed.

Seculink

Keeps the case together against any drop.

Explore its development history

When a foldable opens flat, a gap can form along the spine, right where the two halves of a case meet. Seculink, new for iPhone Duo, joins those halves with a link structure that moves with the hinge as it folds and sits flush when the phone lies flat, so the spine stays covered at every angle. Patent pending.
