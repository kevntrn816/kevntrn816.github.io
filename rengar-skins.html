Game camera puts the model at roughly the pitch the client renders at, so you can see what survives the angle — how much of the chest piece you ever actually see, whether the silhouette still reads. It is the angle rather than the scale: in a real game the champion is around a tenth of the screen's height, which is smaller than this panel can usefully show.

The ability footage is base Rengar. Riot publishes it per champion rather than per skin, so this page cannot tell you what a given skin's Q looks like — that is still the one question a showcase video answers better.
